*** 5.3.6

Adapt utlc rules to describe the other three strategies for evaluation—full beta-reduction, normal-order, and lazy evaluation.

call-by-value:

#+begin_src
t_1 -> t_1'
-------------------         (E-App1)
t_1 t_2 -> t_1' t_2

t_2 -> t_2'
-------------------         (E-App2)
v_1 t_2 -> v_1 t_2'


(λx.t_1) v -> [x ↦ v]t_1   (E-AppAbs)
#+end_src

`E-App1` defines the order of evalating `t u v w` is `((t u) v) w`.

full beta-reduction:

#+begin_src
t_1 -> t_1'
-------------------             (E-App1)
t_1 t_2 -> t_1' t_2

t_2 -> t_2'
-------------------             (E-App2)
t_1 t_2 -> t_1 t_2'


(λx.t_1) t_2 -> [x ↦ t_2]t_1    (E-AppAbs)

t_1 -> t_1'
-----------------               (E-Body)
λx.t_1 -> λx.t_1'
#+end_src

`E-App1` and `E-App2` implies arbitrary evaluation order of beta-reduction. Or, associativity of reduction operation on two terms.

normal-order:

#+begin_src
t_1 -> t_1'
-------------------         (E-App1)
t_1 t_2 -> t_1' t_2


v_1 -> λx.t_1
-----------------------     (E-AppAbs)
v_1 t_2 -> [x ↦ t_2]t_1

t -> t'
-------------               (E-Body)
λx.t -> λx.t'
#+end_src

lazy evaluation:

#+begin_src
t_1 -> t_1'
-------------------             (E-App1)
t_1 t_2 -> t_1' t_2


(λx.t_1) t_2 -> [x ↦ t_2]t_1    (E-AppAbs)
#+end_src
